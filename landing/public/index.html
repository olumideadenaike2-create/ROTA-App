Questions

What churches ask us

Do volunteers need to download an app?

No. They tap the link in their text and see their role. They can add it to their home screen if they want quick access, but they don't have to.

We already use ChurchSuite or Planning Center. Can we use both?

Yes. TeamRota only does rotas and check-in, so it works alongside your church management system. Import from a spreadsheet or CSV now; direct sync is planned for the Large plan.

What happens if we run out of texts?

Texts pause automatically, so you're never charged for more. Volunteers still get every change by email and app alert. Top up whenever you like.

Who can see volunteers' phone numbers?

Only church admins and the heads of the department a volunteer serves in. Volunteers can see who's in their unit, but not their phone numbers. We never sell your data.

Does check-in work if the venue Wi‑Fi is poor?

Yes. A check-in is saved on the phone and syncs as soon as signal comes back.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Join early access and we'll set up your church with a free trial and help importing your volunteers.
